# Quantum Systems

Papers on observation-based quantum-system forecasting and compatible cross-family or partial-family transfer.

[Back to the main README](../README.md)

## Papers

### [Data-Driven Quantum-System Prediction with WPM](Quantum_Physics_Prediction.pdf)

Develops observation-based forecasts for wave, lattice, spin, light-matter, driven, and open quantum systems. It distinguishes physical measurement probabilities from uncertainty about their future evolution and preserves relevant physical constraints.

### [Quantum Cross-Family and Partial-Family Prediction](Quantum_Cross_and_Partial_Family.pdf)

Transfers compatible normalized observables and targets between quantum families. Component eligibility, clock and size normalization, calibration, and abstention support partial transfer without assuming shared state spaces or universal dynamics.
